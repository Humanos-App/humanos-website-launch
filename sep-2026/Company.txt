WRITING / DESIGN RULES
==================================================

The Company page should feel like a category-defining infrastructure thesis, not a product feature page.

The visitor should understand three things by the end:

1. AI creates continuously changing risk.
2. That risk needs a persistent, verifiable reputation.
3. The reputation becomes dramatically more valuable when it can be reused across a network.

Do NOT spend significant space explaining SDKs, APIs, individual guardrails, KYA implementation, human authorization workflows, pricing or detailed Risk Intelligence features. Those belong on the product pages.

Do NOT use vague language such as:
"making AI safer"
"building trust in AI"
"responsible AI"
"AI governance platform"

Be concrete about risk, economic activity, reputation and network effects.

Most importantly, make PORTABILITY and REUSE the central differentiator:

A Risk Score built in one place can create value somewhere else.

A control verified in one place can become evidence somewhere else.

A history accumulated with one participant can be trusted by another.

That is why Humanos is a NETWORK rather than simply another risk-scoring or guardrails product.

- off-white background
- black typography
- purple accent
- monospace labels
- thin borders
- generous whitespace
- technical/network visuals
- very little decorative imagery

Reduce total copy wherever possible. Strong headlines and diagrams should carry the story.
